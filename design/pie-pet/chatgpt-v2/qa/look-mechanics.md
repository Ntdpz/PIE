# PIE look mechanics

The seated cross-legged lower body, feet, hands and torso stay anchored. The head
leads by natural yaw and pitch; dog muzzle/nose tip and the complete original eyes
turn with it. Ears follow a little. No rotated whole-sprite turntable or replacement
pupils. The camera remains fixed and the face remains readable.

Screen coordinates: 000 up, 090 screen-right, 180 down, 270 screen-left. Up raises
the chin/nose and directs both eyes upward; down tucks the chin and lowers gaze.
Right/left shift nose and face surface clearly to that side. Diagonals combine
both axes as even clockwise steps.

The cross clip is on PIE's anatomical right temple (viewer-left when frontal).
It must follow the same head surface, and may become naturally occluded in a left
profile. Never copy it onto the opposite near-side temple to imitate mirroring.

Keep the same seated-body registration, scale, dog-ear/muzzle construction, pink
and navy polo, dark trousers and white shoes. No props, symbols or detached effects.
